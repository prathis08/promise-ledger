# Statements by Narendra Modi / Amit Shah about international indices

Status: DRAFT, researched 2026-10-17. Wording is from press reports unless noted; verbatim text and dates were NOT checked against PIB transcripts. Verify before use in any claim file.

## Narendra Modi

### Ease of Doing Business (World Bank) - cites the ranking
- Nov 2017 (after India rose from 130 to 100): reported as "World Bank ranking is recognition for our work". Reported remark: "Some people do not understand the impact of India's ranking going up from 142 to 100"; he also said earlier governments knew the World Bank well and were now questioning the ranking. Sources: https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/world-bank-ranking-is-recognition-for-our-work-modi.html ; https://www.pressreader.com/india/the-sunday-guardian/20171105/281930248250095 ; https://www.pressreader.com/india/the-free-press-journal/20171104/281663960277147
- Jamnagar rally, Gujarat (date not given in the result): said his government scrapped about 2,000 obsolete laws and India's ease-of-doing-business position rose from 142 to 63 in five years; wants India in the top 50. Source: https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fmy-government-scrapped-2000-obsolete-laws-helped-improve-india-spot-in-ease-of-doing-business-list-pm-modi-1152406.html
- Note: the Doing Business report was discontinued in 2021 after data irregularities (not affecting India specifically, but DB2018 and DB2020 data were reviewed).
- Related: Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal lauding the PM: https://embed.wionews.com/india-news/piyush-goyal-lauds-pm-for-path-breaking-improvements-in-indias-business-environment-257875

### Global Innovation Index (WIPO) - cites the ranking
- Virtual interaction with startups, Jan 2022: "India's GII ranking improved from 81 in 2015 to 46 now". Sources: https://www.dtnext.in/news/national/india-global-innovation-index-ranking-improved-from-81-in-2015-to-46-now-pm-modi ; https://organiser.org/2022/01/15/14380/bharat/india-s-global-innovation-index-ranking-improved-from-81-in-2015-to-46-now-prime-minister-modi/
- Smart India Hackathon, March 2019: said India gained 24 places in four years and aims for the top 25 (cited an 81 baseline). Source: https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/india-keen-to-reach-top-25-position-in-global-innovation-index-soon-modi-119030200780_1.html ; https://www.dnaindia.com/business/report-india-keen-to-reach-top-25-position-in-global-innovation-index-soon-pm-modi-2725822
- 2019 (event per EurasianTimes): cited a rise from 76 (2014) to 57 (2018). Source: https://www.eurasiantimes.com/indias-rank-in-global-innovation-index-reaches-56-in-2018-pm-modi/amp (headline says 56; WIPO lists 57 for 2018 - check wording).
- Baselines differ between speeches (81 in 2014 vs 81 in 2015). WIPO lists 76 for 2014 and 81 for 2015.
